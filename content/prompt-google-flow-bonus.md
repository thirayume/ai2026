## ✅ Master Prompt พร้อมใช้

```text
Create ONE completely original 10-second cinematic live-action video.

The prompt is intentionally shared with many different creators.

Your job is NOT to reproduce a common or predictable interpretation.

Your job is to invent a fresh, unexpected Thai story every time this prompt is generated.

CORE IDEA:

Start with an ordinary moment that could genuinely happen in Thailand.

Then, within the same continuous shot, something completely unexpected happens.

The unexpected event must be original, visually clear, and different from predictable AI-video clichés.

THAI WORLD LOCK:

The video must unmistakably feel like contemporary Thailand.

Use authentic Thai people, natural Thai behavior, Thai environments, Thai architecture, Thai streets, Thai vehicles, Thai clothing, Thai objects, Thai atmosphere, and realistic Thai environmental details.

However:

DO NOT force specific Thai landmarks.

DO NOT always use Bangkok.

DO NOT always use markets.

DO NOT always use temples.

DO NOT always use tuk-tuks.

DO NOT always use monks.

DO NOT always use street food.

DO NOT use stereotypical Thailand imagery unless it naturally belongs to the story.

The location must be independently chosen for each generation.

CREATIVE DIVERSITY ENGINE:

Before creating the video, independently invent a unique combination of:

1. A Thai everyday situation

2. A specific but non-famous location

3. One or more original characters

4. One unexpected object, detail, or environmental element

5. A unique emotional tone

6. A strange event

7. A visual transformation or consequence

8. An unexpected final image

These elements must work together as ONE believable story.

Do not reuse the most obvious combination.

Avoid predictable concepts such as:

generic flying cars,
generic giant monsters,
generic portals,
generic explosions,
generic zombies,
generic aliens,
generic superheroes,
generic time travel,
generic ghosts appearing suddenly,
or scenes copied from famous movies.

If the first idea feels obvious, discard it and create a more original idea.

STORY STRUCTURE:

0–3 SECONDS:

Begin completely ordinary.

The viewer should initially believe they are watching a real moment from everyday life in Thailand.

3–7 SECONDS:

Introduce something impossible.

The change must happen naturally inside the existing environment.

Do not suddenly cut to another location.

Do not reset the scene.

Do not introduce the impossible event without visual cause or continuity.

7–10 SECONDS:

Deliver an unexpected visual payoff.

The ending should make the viewer think:

“Wait… what just happened?”

The final image should be strong enough to make the viewer replay the video.

IMPORTANT:

The story must be visually understandable without explanation.

Do not explain the mystery.

Do not use narration.

Do not use explanatory text.

Do not reveal the ending early.

CONTINUOUS REALITY:

Everything must exist within one continuous physical environment.

Objects maintain consistent scale, position, lighting, and physics.

Characters maintain consistent appearance, clothing, age, and identity throughout the shot.

No random character changes.

No random costume changes.

No location teleportation.

No impossible camera jumps.

HUMAN PERFORMANCE:

Use authentic Thai human behavior.

Natural posture.
Natural walking.
Natural eye movement.
Natural breathing.
Subtle facial reactions.
Micro-expressions.
Realistic hesitation.
Believable surprise.

Avoid exaggerated acting.

CINEMATOGRAPHY:

Photorealistic live-action.

High-end cinematic realism.

Professional cinema camera.

Natural camera movement motivated by the action.

Realistic lens characteristics.

Natural depth of field.

Physically accurate lighting.

Realistic shadows and reflections.

Detailed skin texture.

Detailed environmental textures.

Natural motion blur.

Realistic physics.

The camera must feel like a real camera physically present in Thailand.

SOUND:

Create immersive location sound appropriate to the chosen Thai environment.

Use realistic Thai environmental ambience.

Traffic, motorcycles, rain, insects, birds, market sounds, distant conversations, wind, footsteps, machinery, or other sounds may appear naturally when appropriate.

Do not use narration.

Dialogue is optional.

If dialogue naturally occurs, it must be short and spoken in natural Thai.

Each line must be spoken by the correct character.

Thai pronunciation must be clear, natural, and intelligible.

Do not force dialogue if the scene works better without it.

VISUAL RESTRICTIONS:

No subtitles.
No captions.
No explanatory text.
No logos.
No watermark.
No UI.
No title cards.

ORIGINALITY RULE:

This is a public AI video challenge.

Assume thousands of people may generate this exact prompt.

Therefore, deliberately avoid the most obvious interpretation.

Do not attempt to create the same story another creator would likely generate.

Do not copy existing viral AI videos.

Do not imitate any movie, television show, anime, game, advertisement, music video, or famous cinematic sequence.

Create an independently imagined story.

FINAL CREATIVE RULE:

The beginning must feel familiar.

The middle must feel impossible.

The ending must feel unforgettable.

But the actual story, characters, location, object, emotion, and impossible event must be freely invented.

ONE PROMPT.

ONE RULE.

ONE 10-SECOND VIDEO.

INFINITE POSSIBILITIES.

TECHNICAL:

Duration: 10 seconds
Aspect Ratio: 9:16
Resolution: 1080×1920 or higher
Frame Rate: 24fps
Photorealistic live action
Cinematic lighting
Natural motion blur
Realistic physics
Professional cinema camera
Immersive spatial sound
```

## วิธีใช้กับ Google Flow

1. เปิด Google Flow
2. สร้าง Video Generation ใหม่
3. ตั้งแนวตั้ง **9:16**
4. Copy Master Prompt ทั้งชุดด้านบน
5. Generate โดยไม่ตัดส่วน `CREATIVE DIVERSITY ENGINE`
6. Generate ซ้ำหลายรอบด้วย Prompt เดิมเพื่อดู Creative Diversity
7. เปรียบเทียบผลลัพธ์ด้าน:
   - Thai everyday situation
   - non-famous location
   - characters
   - strange event
   - transformation
   - final image
8. เลือก Output ที่ไม่ stereotype และมี Continuity ดีที่สุด

> **Workshop Rule:** ทุกทีมใช้ Prompt เดียวกัน ห้ามเปลี่ยน Master Prompt ก่อน Generate รอบแรก
